A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Kenner Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Kenner, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Kenner

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
